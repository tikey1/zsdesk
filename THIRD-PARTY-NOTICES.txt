NOTE ON ZSUI (MPL-2.0):
The upstream qiu7824/zsdesk originally bundled its UI layer "ZSUI" under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0. This fork's design intent is to REPLACE ZSUI with
ghboke/core-ui (MIT). ZSUI is still an ACTIVE build/runtime dependency of this
fork: it is referenced as a path dependency (e.g. ../../../zsui) from
crates/zsdesk-app/Cargo.toml and crates/zsdesk-ui/Cargo.toml, pinned in
Cargo.lock as zsui 0.3.0-alpha.2, and its types are still imported by
crates/zsdesk-ui. (The zsui source directory is not vendored into this tree, but
the dependency is live.) Therefore the MPL-2.0 obligation for those ZSUI files
applies to the distributed product TODAY, and the notice
(packaging/licenses/ZSUI-MPL-2.0.txt) remains in force. This fork becomes pure
MIT only after the zsui dependency is removed from Cargo.toml and the source
imports (crates/zsdesk-ui) — not merely by replacing the UI in crates/zsdesk-coreui.
